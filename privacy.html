CAD Touch Lite

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Publisher: Marko Nedic software

This policy covers all versions of the app: CAD Touch Lite for Windows from the Microsoft Store, the Android app from Google Play and from Huawei AppGallery, and CAD Touch for iPhone and iPad from the App Store (the same app also runs on Mac computers with Apple silicon). Where the versions differ, the paragraph starts with "Windows", "Android" or "iPhone and iPad".

Summary

Your drawings are opened and processed on your own device. On Android, part of a drawing is sent to our server only if you start an AI drawing review yourself (see "AI drawing review"). The iPhone and iPad app does not collect, store or share any personal data, and neither does CAD Touch Lite for Windows before version 1.2. CAD Touch Lite for Windows from version 1.2 can send basic usage statistics, never any drawing content (see "Windows usage statistics"). The Android app (from version 1.9) can send basic usage statistics, never any drawing content (see "Android usage statistics"), and from version 1.11 it downloads promotion settings (see "Promotion settings"). Version 2.0 of the Android app from Google Play contains Google's advertising software, which is switched off at the release of version 2.0 and sends nothing while it is off; what would change if ads are switched on is described under "Ads on Android (version 2.0 and newer)". The Huawei AppGallery version contains Huawei's advertising software (Huawei Ads Kit, Petal Ads), which is also switched off and sends nothing for ads while it is off; it contains neither Google AdMob nor Google's consent software (see "Huawei AppGallery version").

Apart from the Android and Windows statistics, the download of promotion settings and, only if ads are switched on, the ads software (Google's ads and consent software, see "Ads on Android (version 2.0 and newer)"; in the AppGallery version, Huawei's Ads Kit, see "Huawei AppGallery version"), the app itself sends nothing unless you choose to share something or start an AI drawing review, except what the app stores need for purchases: on Android, Google Play (in the AppGallery version: Huawei) handles the subscription, and in versions that offer it, Google's billing component inside the app also sends technical diagnostic data to Google; in the AppGallery version, Huawei's HMS Core SDK sends technical data about its own work to Huawei; on iPhone and iPad, the App Store handles the subscription (see "Purchases and subscriptions"). None of this reaches us, except the data of an AI drawing review or AI chat you start, the credit purchases and Pro subscription that our server checks with Google Play (see "AI drawing review"), and feedback you choose to send (see "Feedback and problem reports"); otherwise, when something is bought, we only receive the stores' sales reports, described below.

Your drawings

Windows: DWG and DXF files you open are read locally. When a DWG file is opened for the first time, a converted DXF copy is saved in your Documents folder (Documents\CAD Touch Lite) so it can be reopened quickly. You can delete these copies at any time from the app or from File Explorer. No drawing, file name or file content is ever uploaded anywhere.

Android: You open a drawing (DWG or DXF, and from version 1.11 also a PDF plan or a picture of a plan in JPG or PNG) with Android's file picker, or from another app with "Open with" or "Share"; the app gets access only to the files you choose. From version 1.11 you can also use "Add folder": you choose one folder in Android's folder picker, the app remembers the access that Android gives it to that folder (this is stored only on your device) and adds the drawings, PDFs and pictures it finds in that folder and its subfolders, also new ones later. When you remove the folder in Settings › Drawing folders, the app gives that access back. So that you can reopen it and keep notes on it, the app normally saves a copy of the file in its own folder on your device (the location is shown in the app under About) and keeps a prepared copy in its cache so that it opens again quickly. The original file is only read, never changed. Drawings are read entirely on your device: no drawing, file name or file content is uploaded anywhere, except the parts you choose to send in an AI drawing review that you start yourself (see "AI drawing review"). If you open a file from a cloud drive, that drive's app downloads it; CAD Touch Lite itself does not connect to the internet to open drawings.

iPhone and iPad: You open a drawing with the Files picker, or from another app with "Open in" or "Share"; the app gets access only to the file you choose. The app saves a copy of the file in its own folder, which you can see in the Files app under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) › CAD Touch › Drawings, and keeps a prepared copy in its cache so that it opens again quickly. The original file is only read, never changed. Drawings are read entirely on your device: no drawing, file name or file content is uploaded anywhere. If you open a file from iCloud Drive or another cloud service, that service downloads it; CAD Touch itself does not connect to the internet to open drawings.

Notes and pictures

Windows: Notes you pin to a drawing, and the pictures you attach to them, are stored only on your device, in a folder beside the converted drawing (Documents\CAD Touch Lite). "Take photo" opens the Windows Camera app; the app itself does not use the camera. A note leaves your device only when you choose Share, Print or Save, and then it goes only where you send it. Removing a drawing from the app moves its notes to the Recycle Bin.

Android: Notes and their pictures are stored only on your device, in the app's folder beside the drawing copy. "Take photo" opens your device's camera app, which hands the photo to CAD Touch Lite; the app has no camera permission and cannot use the camera by itself (some camera apps also keep their own copy in your gallery). "Add picture" opens Android's photo picker, and the app receives only the pictures you select. The app keeps its own copy of each picture as a JPEG without location (GPS) or other metadata. If a photo cannot be added to a note, the app keeps it in your gallery folder Pictures/CAD Touch (on Android 8 and 9, in the app's folder) and tells you where it is. A note leaves your device only when you choose Share, Print, Save picture, Save as PDF or Copy text, and then it goes only where you send it. Drawings you remove from the app, and pictures you delete, go to the app's own trash and are deleted permanently after 30 days.

iPhone and iPad: Notes and their pictures are stored only on your device, in the app's Drawings folder beside the drawing copy. "Take photo" uses the camera inside the app, so the first time iOS asks whether CAD Touch may use the camera; the app uses it only while you take a photo for a note, and you can turn this off at any time in Settings › CAD Touch. "Add picture" opens Apple's photo picker, which needs no permission: the app receives only the pictures you select and cannot see the rest of your photo library. The app keeps its own copy of each picture as a JPEG without location (GPS) or other metadata, and it does not save photos to your photo library. A note leaves your device only when you choose Share, Print, Save picture, Save as PDF or Copy text, and then it goes only where you send it. Drawings you remove from the app go to the app's own trash and are deleted permanently after 30 days.

Measurements

Android, iPhone and iPad: Measurements you keep on a drawing (distances, perimeters, areas, diameters and point coordinates, in versions that save them) are stored only on your device, in the app's folder beside the drawing copy, together with that drawing's notes. They are saved as a small text file with the measured points in the drawing's own coordinates; the drawing file itself is never changed. Measurements that are not saved (without Pro the app keeps only one, and never saves it) stay on the screen only while the drawing is open and are never written to storage. Measurements are not uploaded anywhere. You can tap a measurement and delete it, or delete all measurements of a drawing; removing a drawing from the app moves its saved measurements to the app's trash together with its notes, where they are deleted permanently after 30 days.

Data we collect

Windows: Before version 1.2: none. From version 1.2 only the usage statistics described under "Windows usage statistics (version 1.2 and newer)". The app contains no advertising or crash-reporting services, does no tracking across apps or websites, and does not require an account with us.

Android: No ads at present and no account with us (version 2.0 can show ads to free users if they are switched on later, see "Ads on Android (version 2.0 and newer)"). From version 3.1.3, if you start an AI drawing review, the parts of the drawing you choose are sent for analysis, and your free check and credits are kept on our server; from version 3.1.3, the project memory for the AI chat is kept on our server for at most 90 days after you last use it, while the conversation stays on your device, and feedback you choose to send is kept on our server (see "AI drawing review" and "Feedback and problem reports"). From version 1.9 the app can send basic usage statistics, which you can turn off (see "Android usage statistics" below); earlier versions send none. From version 1.11 the app also downloads promotion settings from Google Firebase Remote Config, whatever your statistics choice (see "Promotion settings" below). Versions that offer the Pro subscription contain the Google Play Billing Library, a component made by Google, which sends technical diagnostic data about the billing component to Google (see "Purchases and subscriptions"). Because Google counts data that a component inside an app sends as data collected by that app, the app's Data safety section on Google Play lists it as diagnostics. We do not receive it. Separately, if you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Android may report crashes and freezes of your apps, including this one, to Google, and Google shows developers these reports in Google Play Console. That is a setting of your device, not part of the app.

iPhone and iPad: None. The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services and no third-party code that sends data; the app's own code makes no internet connections. Purchases go through Apple's StoreKit, which is part of iOS (see "Purchases and subscriptions"). The app's App Privacy section on the App Store therefore says "Data Not Collected". If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us in anonymous form; that is a setting of your device, not part of the app.

Android test versions: If you take part in a test of the Android app, we know the e-mail address of the Google account that you gave us for the test list or that joined our test group, and we use it only to run the test. Google Play tells testers that it collects certain data about their use of a test version and shares it with the developer; in Google Play Console we see statistics such as installs and crashes, and the feedback you choose to send us.

Purchases and subscriptions

Windows: Subscriptions, the free trial and the one-time purchase, when they become available, are sold and managed by the Microsoft Store. Payment information is handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The app only asks the Microsoft Store whether you own one of them; we do not receive your payment details.

Android: Measuring and notes are the paid Pro features. When the subscription is available in the version you have (test versions may have everything unlocked), it is sold and managed by Google Play, and payment information is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account has an active subscription and stores the answer on your device (whether Pro is active and when this was last checked), so that measuring and notes keep working without a connection: a confirmed subscription keeps Pro working for up to 35 days after the last successful check with Google Play. A new installation that has never received an answer from Google Play, because Google Play is missing, switched off or out of date on the device, has Pro for the first 14 days from the first start; after that, measuring and notes are locked until the first answer. Temporary errors never lock Pro. We do not receive your name, e-mail address or payment details. You can cancel the subscription at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. From version 3.1.3, Pro also includes credits for AI drawing review; to add them, our server checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active (see "AI drawing review").

Android: Google Play gives every developer reports about the sales of their apps. For each order they contain the order number, date, product, price and taxes, the device model and the buyer's country, and for some orders the region, city and postal code. They contain no names or e-mail addresses. We use these reports only for accounting and taxes and keep them as long as tax and accounting rules require. If you contact us about a purchase and give us its order number or the e-mail address of your Google account, we can look up that order in Google Play Console, for example to refund it.

iPhone and iPad: Measuring and notes are the paid Pro features. The subscription is sold and managed by Apple through the App Store, with your Apple Account; payment information is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. The app asks the App Store whether your Apple Account has an active subscription and stores the answer on your device (whether Pro is active and when this was last checked), so that measuring and notes keep working for up to 35 days without a connection to the App Store. We do not receive your name, e-mail address or payment details. Apple gives developers sales reports with totals per product, country and date, without names or contact details. Refunds are requested from and decided by Apple. You can cancel the subscription at any time in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Android: Versions that offer the subscription contain the Google Play Billing Library, Google's component for purchases in apps. It sends technical diagnostic data about its own work to Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Google does not publish exactly what this data contains. The app never passes your drawings, notes, measurements or pictures to it. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive it and cannot see or delete it.

Android: Versions without the subscription ask for no permissions at all. Versions with the subscription have these permissions, all of which Google's billing component requires: "Google Play billing service", "full network access" and "view network connections" (used by Google's billing component, from version 1.9 by Google Analytics for Firebase to send the usage statistics described below, and from version 1.11 by Firebase Remote Config to download promotion settings; from version 3.1.3 also by the app's own code, only to reach our server for an AI drawing review that you start, the free check, your credits, the AI chat about a project, a suggestion you choose to report and feedback you choose to send; the app's own code makes no other internet connections), plus one internal permission that only this app itself can use. iPhone and iPad: The only permission the app asks for is the camera, and only when you tap "Take photo". It does not ask for access to your photo library, location, contacts, microphone or other apps' files.

Settings stored on your device

Windows: The app stores a few local settings (chosen language, measurement units per drawing, list of recently opened drawings) and a cache that lets drawings you have already opened reopen faster. All of it is kept in your user profile and never leaves your device. From version 1.2 the app also stores a random statistics identifier, your statistics choice and any events waiting to be sent (see Windows usage statistics).

Android: The app stores its settings (language, display unit, units per drawing, light or dark background), the list of drawings in its folder, from version 1.11 your favourite and recently opened drawings, the folders you added with "Add folder", how often you tapped a locked Pro feature, how often each promotion was shown, and whether you chose "Notify me" for the AI plan (only a mark on your device: the app asks for no e-mail address and sends no notification), the place you were looking at while another app is in front, a few technical notes (for example how long drawings took to load, to estimate the next loading time) and, in versions with the subscription, whether Pro is active. From version 3.1.3 it also stores the installation ID for AI drawing review, the last credit balance it received and the job numbers of checks whose findings have not been fetched yet; from version 3.1.3 also your AI chat conversations about a project. All of it stays in the app's storage on your device. The app does not use Android's cloud backup. Backup (Android): if you choose a backup folder in Settings › Backup, the app copies your drawings' notes and markups (.cadtouch files) to that folder; if the folder is in a cloud service (for example Google Drive), the copy is stored there under that service's terms. We do not access that folder or your cloud account, and nothing is sent to us. If you move to a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer (Android 12 and newer), your drawings, notes, measurements, pictures and settings can be copied to the new phone as part of that transfer.

iPhone and iPad: The app stores its settings (display unit, units per drawing, light or dark background), the list of drawings in its folder, the place you were looking at if iOS closes the app in the background, a few technical notes (for example how long drawings took to load) and whether Pro is active. The language is the one you choose for the app in iOS Settings or in the app. All of it stays in the app's storage on your device. If you back up your device to iCloud or to a computer, iOS includes the app's drawings, notes, measurements, pictures and settings in that backup, as it does for other apps; caches are not backed up.

Android usage statistics (version 1.9 and newer)

Your choice. For users in the European Union and European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Android app asks the first time you start it whether you allow usage statistics. Until you answer, and if you decline, nothing is sent. Elsewhere, usage statistics are on by default. Everyone can turn them off at any time in the app under Settings; turning it off stops sending and tells the service to stop collecting. The app works the same either way.

What is sent. Events such as: first opening of the app, introduction finished, drawing opened (whether it was the built-in demo drawing, your own drawing, a PDF or a picture, without saying which file), the first time you open a drawing of your own, a drawing that could not be opened (only the kind of error, the file type and a rough size class, never the file name), note saved (with a photo or without), report sent, a locked Pro feature tapped, Pro offer shown or closed, plan tapped, trial started, purchase, a purchase that did not go through and the reason given by Google Play or, in the AppGallery version, by Huawei (for example cancelled or no connection), a pending purchase, trial ended and subscription cancelled. The service also adds technical data that Google Analytics for Firebase collects by itself: the app version, Android version, device model, language, approximate region derived from the network address (the address itself is not stored), and an identifier of this app installation. Drawing content, drawing, file or folder names, the text of notes, names of contractors, photos, coordinates, layer names, measurements, your e-mail address or your Google account. The statistics never use the Android advertising ID and are never used for personalised ads or remarketing. CAD Touch itself never receives the advertising ID; see "Ads on Android (version 2.0 and newer)" for the advertising ID that Google's ads software may read once ads are switched on.

Measuring our ads. Outside the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the events "trial started" and "purchase" are also shared with Google Ads as conversions, only so that we can see which of our ad campaigns lead to a trial or purchase. Where you are asked (EU/EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland), the legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the equivalent UK and Swiss rules), which you can withdraw at any time as described above; withdrawing does not affect what was sent before. Elsewhere, we rely on our legitimate interest in improving the app (or the equivalent basis under the rules of your country), and you can object at any time by turning the statistics off.

Google (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and the United Kingdom, Google LLC elsewhere) processes the data on our behalf as a processor, through Firebase and Google Analytics, under Google's terms for data processing. Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates. See Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and security in Firebase.

After that only aggregated reports without the installation identifier remain. Because the statistics are tied to an installation identifier, we cannot look up a person by name; to have the data of your installation deleted, write to us; clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes the installation identifier from the device, so later events can no longer be linked to the earlier ones.

Promotion settings (version 1.11 and newer). The Android app downloads promotion settings from Google Firebase Remote Config, for example the dates and text of a discount campaign. We rely on our legitimate interest in showing current offers in the app (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Google processes this data on our behalf, as described under "Who processes it".

Updates while the app is open (version 2.0 and newer). While the app is on screen, it listens for changes of the promotion settings through Firebase Remote Config, so that changed settings (for example a message, the end of a promotion or the ad switches) usually apply within seconds. When you leave the app, it stops listening for changes and opens no new connection; Firebase or Android then closes the open one. The app also fetches the settings when it comes on screen and about once an hour while it stays open (at most once an hour). Like the download itself, this does not depend on the statistics choice, uses the Firebase installation identifier, and sends no statistics event.

iPhone and iPad: no usage statistics and no tracking.

Windows usage statistics (version 1.2 and newer)

These statistics apply only to CAD Touch Lite for Windows from version 1.2. Earlier Windows versions send nothing.

What is sent. The app sends usage events to Google Analytics 4 (the same Google Analytics property as the Android app, in the data stream "Windows app") using the GA4 Measurement Protocol: a secure HTTPS request sent directly to Google. Each event carries a random identifier (a randomly generated GUID stored on your computer; it is not a device ID and not an account ID), the platform ("windows"), the app version and a session identifier. Events are: first opening of the app, drawing opened (whether it was the built-in demo drawing, your own drawing, a PDF or a picture, and the file type such as DWG or PDF, without saying which file), the first time you open a drawing of your own, a drawing that could not be opened (only the kind of error, the file type and a rough size class in MB, never the file name), note saved (with a photo or without), report sent (printed, saved as PDF, shared, or one report per person; never names), measurement finished (which tool you used and on which kind of file: CAD drawing, PDF or picture; never the measured values), scale set, Pro offer shown (and from which place in the app), plan tapped, trial started and purchase (with the Microsoft Store identifier of the plan), a purchase that did not go through (with the reason), and a locked Pro feature tapped (which feature). The request contains no device or Windows details: no location, no user ID and a fixed app name instead of a browser identifier. Because the random identifier is pseudonymous, we treat these statistics as personal data and do not call them anonymous.

What is never sent. Drawing content, drawing or file names, the text of notes, photos, your files, your e-mail address or any other personal data. The statistics are never used for personalised ads or remarketing.

Your choice. In the European Union and European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before anything is sent; until you agree, and if you decline, nothing is sent. Elsewhere, usage statistics are on until you switch them off. Everyone can turn them off at any time in the app under Settings; the app then stops sending. The app works the same either way. Without an internet connection, events are kept on your computer and sent later, only if sending is allowed. We can also switch sending off for all users through a setting the app reads from https://cadtouch.app/config/windows.json only while sending is allowed (for example {"analytics":false}); this request contains no usage events.

Purpose and legal basis. The same as for Android: we use the statistics to see how many people open the app and where they stop, so that we can improve it. Where you are asked (EU/EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland), the legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the equivalent UK and Swiss rules), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings; withdrawing does not affect what was sent before. Elsewhere, we rely on our legitimate interest in improving the app (or the equivalent basis under the rules of your country), and you can object at any time by turning the statistics off.

Who processes it. Google (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and the United Kingdom, Google LLC elsewhere) processes the data on our behalf as a processor, through Google Analytics, under Google's terms for data processing. Data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates. See Google Privacy Policy.

How long. As for Android, event-level data is kept in Google Analytics for 2 months; after that only aggregated reports remain. We cannot look up a person by name; to have the data tied to your random identifier deleted, write to us. Uninstalling the app or deleting its local data removes the identifier from your computer, so later events can no longer be linked to the earlier ones.

Network address. As with any internet connection, Google receives your computer's network (IP) address when events are sent, and the host of cadtouch.app receives it when the app reads its settings file. The app does not add any other location data.

PDF, pictures and measuring on Windows (version 1.2). Version 1.2 adds a PDF reader, measuring on PDF plans and pictures, and an image gallery. Text search in a PDF uses Windows' own text recognition (OCR) on your computer, without the internet. All of this is local: your files, PDFs and pictures never leave your computer.

Ads on Android (version 2.0 and newer)

Now: ads are switched off. The Android app contains Google's Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (consent) SDK. While ads are switched off, as at the release of version 2.0, both stay inactive: the app starts no ads software, asks for no ad consent, makes no ad requests and makes no contact with Google's advertising or consent servers. The permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID lets Google's ads software read the Android advertising ID if ads are switched on later; it is never used while ads are off. We can switch ads on with a setting sent through Firebase Remote Config, without an app update. Everything below applies only after ads have been switched on; the date at the top of this page shows when this policy was last changed.

If ads are switched on: who sees them. Ads served by Google AdMob may be shown only to users without Pro and not in the free trial, and only after Google Play has told the app that. They may appear as a banner at the bottom of the home screen (never over a drawing), as a full-screen ad on the home screen after you close a drawing (not on every close and not more than a few times a day), and as an ad you can choose to watch in exchange for a small reward in the Pro-feature window (one more note on that drawing, one area or perimeter measurement, one PDF export of the drawing, or your letterhead on one document; the reward is given only after the ad was watched). Pro subscribers and users in the free trial never see ads, never get the ad consent form, and buying Pro removes the banner at once. The state of the rewards (daily counts and single-use rewards) stays on your device and is sent nowhere.

What Google's ads software collects. AI drawing review (Android, version 3.1.3 and newer)

Only when you start it. AI drawing review is an optional feature of the Android app. Nothing is sent until you choose the sheet or area, see what will be sent and how many credits it will use, and tap Send. If you never use it, nothing described in this section happens, apart from the installation ID (see below) and the statistics events described below. The Windows app and the iPhone and iPad app do not offer AI drawing review.

What is sent. For each sheet or area you choose (of a DWG or DXF drawing, a PDF page or a picture of a plan): images of that area of the drawing (one image of the whole area and enlarged parts of it), and, for DWG and DXF drawings, the texts and dimensions in that area with their positions. If you check several drawings together, the app also sends each drawing's file name, so the findings can say which drawing they belong to. If you type an instruction (at most 500 characters), it is sent too. The app also sends technical details of the check: the language you want the findings in, the kind of file, its units, the PDF page number, whether you chose a standard or a fast check, and a random job number that the app creates for this check.

What is never sent in a check or a chat. Other sheets and areas you did not choose, hidden layers, your notes and their photos, contractor names, measurements, your name, e-mail address, Google account, contacts or location.

Who processes it. The data goes over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our own server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc.), and from there to the Claude API of Anthropic (Anthropic, PBC), which analyses it and returns the findings. Both process the data on our behalf as service providers (processors). Anthropic's Commercial Terms say: "Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services", and that data submitted through its services is processed under Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum. Your drawing is not used to train AI models.

Where it is processed. Cloudflare runs our server on its worldwide network, and Anthropic and its cloud providers may process the data in the United States and in other countries. Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, with the UK and Swiss addenda, for transfers of personal data outside the European Economic Area.

Standard and fast checks. A standard check is sent through Anthropic's batch service (Message Batches): it is usually ready within minutes and at the latest within 24 hours (a large area that is checked in parts: at the latest within 48 hours). A fast check is sent to Anthropic directly and is usually ready within a few minutes.

How long it is kept.

  • On our server: the images, texts and your instruction are kept only until the analysis is finished (a fast check: usually a few minutes; a standard check: until Anthropic has finished, at the latest about 49 hours) and are then deleted, except, if you scan the project for the AI chat, the project memory described under "Ask AI about your project". The result (the findings, the job number, the model name, the number of credits and tokens used, and the times) is kept for at most 7 days, so that the app can fetch it even if it was closed, and so that you can unlock all findings of a free check without a new analysis. After 7 days it is deleted automatically. If you cancel a check, its data is deleted at once.
  • At Anthropic, standard check: Anthropic's documentation says that batch processing stores request and response data for up to 29 days after the batch is created. Our server asks Anthropic to delete the batch as soon as it has fetched the result.
  • At Anthropic, fast check: under Anthropic's retention policy for commercial and API customers, inputs and outputs are deleted from Anthropic's systems within 30 days.
  • Exceptions at Anthropic: if Anthropic's automated safety systems flag a request as a possible violation of its Usage Policy, Anthropic may keep the input and output for up to 2 years; it may also keep data where the law requires it.
  • On your device: the findings appear on the drawing as AI suggestions. Each one you accept becomes a normal note and stays until you delete it; suggestions you reject are deleted from the device. The job number is kept beside the drawing until the findings have been fetched.

Free check and credits: how the app is recognised. The first check of one sheet is free once per installation of the app, and further checks use credits. The free check is available only in the app installed from Google Play, on a device that passes Google Play's integrity check. So that this works without an account, and so that the free check cannot be repeated endlessly, the app uses two things:

  • An installation ID: a random code that the app creates the first time you open the AI review or send feedback (it is not your phone's serial number or advertising ID). Your credits are kept on our server under this code.
  • Google Play Integrity: before each request the app asks Google Play to confirm that it is the genuine app from Google Play on a genuine Android device. For this, Google Play processes the app's package name, version and signing certificate, its Google Play licence status, a device attestation token and a digest of the request. Google processes this data under the Google Play Terms of Service; our server receives only Google's answer (for example whether the app and the device passed the check) and uses it only to protect the free check and credits against abuse. Google's Play Integrity terms do not allow it to be used to track people, and we do not use it for that. The free check is tied to the installation ID. We do not receive or store any identifier of your device.

Buying credits. Credits are bought in the app through Google Play's billing system. Payment details are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see your card or bank details, your name or your e-mail address. So that the credits reach the right installation, the app passes the installation ID, as an anonymous code, to Google Play's purchase field, and our server checks each purchase with Google before it adds the credits. If you have Pro, our server also asks Google Play whether your subscription is active and until when, using the subscription's purchase token, so that it can add the credits included with Pro. From Google we receive the order number, the product, the purchase time and whether the purchase is valid, cancelled or refunded. On our server we keep, under your installation ID, your credit balance, your purchases (order number, product, credits, date) and how credits were used (job number, credits, date, sheet size). To apply the limit on cancelled checks described in the terms, our server also counts, under your installation ID, the checks you cancelled; each entry is deleted after 24 hours. Your credit balance is kept as long as it holds credits. Purchase records and the history of credit use are kept for 24 months after your last purchase or check, and in any case as long as your balance still holds credits from those purchases; records needed for accounting and taxes are kept as long as those rules require. If Google refunds a purchase, Google tells our server and the credits from that purchase are removed.

Free check: what you see. The free check analyses the whole area you chose (one page) but shows only 3 findings and the real number of findings of each kind. The other findings are kept on our server for at most 7 days, so that you can unlock them with credits without a new analysis; after that they are deleted.

Ask AI about your project (version 3.1.3 and newer). After a check, you can ask questions about the checked project in a chat.

  • Project scan: before the first question, the app scans the project; this uses credits per page and is shown before you send, like a check. The same data is sent as for a check (see "What is sent") and, for DWG and DXF drawings, also the blocks in that area (their names, attributes, outline and layer) and the names of the visible layers. Only objects on visible layers are sent.
  • Project memory on our server: so that you can keep asking without a new scan, our server keeps a text memory of the project for at most 90 days after you last use it: the blocks (names, attributes, outline and layer), texts and dimensions from visible layers, the names of the visible layers, the findings and a short summary of the project. No images of the drawing are kept. Apart from the project memory, our server keeps only the numbers it needs for your credits. After 90 days without use the project memory is deleted automatically; to continue later, the project has to be scanned again.
  • Questions: each question you type is sent as text, together with the project memory, to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the answer. Anthropic keeps and deletes this data as described above for a fast check. Your questions and the answers are not stored on our server and are not written to its log (unless you report an answer, see "Reporting a suggestion").
  • The conversation (your questions and the answers) is saved only on your device, beside the drawing, until you delete it. The chat always shows the notice "AI answers, may be wrong".

Attachments in the project chat. If you attach a file to a question in "AI about the project" (a PDF, a picture, a DWG/DXF drawing or a text file), the app sends it, together with your question, the conversation and the project's memory, to our server and to Anthropic (Claude) for the analysis. A DWG or DXF drawing is first converted on your phone into pictures and a list of its texts; a picture is made smaller. Nothing is sent before you choose a file and tap to see the price. Until you confirm, our server keeps the prepared file for at most 30 minutes, only to show you the price; if you cancel, it is deleted immediately. After the analysis the file is deleted from our server. Only a short summary written by the AI stays in the project's memory, together with the file's name, for 90 days after its last use; we instruct the AI to leave out personal data from this summary. The file itself is never stored permanently.

Text filter. Before an instruction for a check or a chat question is sent for analysis, its text alone (without the drawing) is checked by an automatic filter that runs on Anthropic's Claude Haiku model. It only decides whether the text is a question or task about the drawing or project. If the filter rejects it, the text is not sent further and no credits are used. Anthropic keeps and deletes the filtered text as described above for a fast check. A rejected text is not stored in the app or on our server; our server keeps only counts (the kind of text, that it was rejected and its length).

Reporting a suggestion. On each AI finding and on each answer in the AI chat there is a button to report it ("Report suggestion", "Report answer"), which sends a message like "Send feedback or report a problem", as described under "Feedback and problem reports", together with the text of that finding or answer and its job number or message ID, but not the drawing.

Statistics. If usage statistics are on for you (see "Android usage statistics"), the app also counts events about the AI review, such as the introduction card being shown, the AI button tapped and a free check started, without any content of the drawing or of the findings.

Legal basis. We process the drawing data, the results, the project memory, your chat questions and your credits to carry out the check, the chat and the purchase you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). We use Play Integrity, the installation ID and the count of cancelled checks for our legitimate interest in protecting the service against abuse (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your choices and deleting this data. You decide every time whether to send a drawing; if you never use AI review, no drawing is sent. Cancelling a check deletes its data on our server at once, results are deleted automatically after 7 days, and project memory for the chat 90 days after its last use.
