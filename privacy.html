CAD Touch Lite

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026 · Publisher: Marko Nedic software

Summary

CAD Touch Lite does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your drawings are opened and processed only on your own device.

Your drawings

DWG and DXF files you open are read locally. When a DWG file is opened for the first time, a converted DXF copy is saved in your Documents folder (Documents\CAD Touch Lite) so it can be reopened quickly. You can delete these copies at any time from the app or from File Explorer. No drawing, file name or file content is ever uploaded anywhere.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services, and it does not require an account with us.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions and the free trial are sold and managed by the Microsoft Store. Payment information is handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The app only asks the Microsoft Store whether your subscription is active; we do not receive your payment details.

Settings stored on your device

The app stores a few local settings (chosen language, measurement units per drawing, list of recently opened drawings) and a cache that lets drawings you have already opened reopen faster. All of it is kept in your user profile and never leaves your device.

Ratings

The app may occasionally ask whether you would like to rate it. The rating window belongs to the Microsoft Store; what you write there is sent to Microsoft, not to us.

This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. As with any website, the hosting provider (GitHub) receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads, and Microsoft receives a request when the "Get it from Microsoft" button image loads.

If you arrive through a link that carries a campaign tag (for example ?cid=…), the tag is added to the Microsoft Store link you click, so that Microsoft's reports can show us, in total numbers only, which source sent visitors. The tag is not stored on your device.

Advertising cookies on this website

These apply only to this website, never to the app, and only if you agree. On your first visit a notice asks whether we may use cookies and similar technologies for advertising. If you choose Decline, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is loaded and nothing is stored except your choice. Declining changes nothing about how the website or the app works.

If you choose Accept, the tags of these advertising companies are loaded:

  • Microsoft Advertising (Universal Event Tracking), Microsoft Corporation. Microsoft collects or receives personal data through this tag; see the Microsoft Privacy Statement. It sets the cookies _uetsid, _uetvid and _uetmsclkid on this website and MUID and MSPTC on bing.com, and Microsoft states that it keeps this data for up to 390 days.
  • Reddit Pixel, Reddit, Inc.; see the Reddit Privacy Policy, which also lists its cookies.
  • Meta Pixel, Meta Platforms; see the Meta Privacy Policy, which also lists its cookies.
  • Google tag for Google Ads, Google LLC; see how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

What they receive: the address of the page you opened and of the page you came from, your IP address, browser and device details, cookie and click identifiers, and whether you clicked a "Get it from Microsoft" button.

Why: to measure whether our advertising works, and to show our ads on those companies' services to people who have visited this website. The companies also use the data to run and protect their own advertising services, as their notices describe. We do not sell personal data.

Legal basis: your consent, both for storing the cookies and for the use of the data.

Where the data goes: these companies are based in the United States and process the data there under the safeguards described in their notices.

How long: your choice is remembered in your browser (an entry named ctl-consent) for six months, then the notice asks again. How long each company keeps its data is stated in its notice.

Changing your mind: "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page reopens the notice. Choosing Decline stops the tags and deletes the cookies they set on this website.

Your rights: you can ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to its use or withdraw your consent at any time, and complain to the data protection authority of your country (in Serbia: the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection). We do not ourselves receive data that identifies you; the companies above hold it, and their notices explain how to exercise your rights with them.

Who is responsible for this website: Marko Nedic software, an independent developer based in Serbia. No data protection officer is appointed.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write on the support page. The page is public, so please do not include personal details you do not want others to see.
