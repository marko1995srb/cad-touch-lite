What the app does

CAD Touch Lite opens, shows and measures DWG and DXF drawings, and lets you pin notes with text and pictures to them. It does not edit drawings: notes are stored separately on your device and the drawing file is never changed. Measurements are calculated from the drawing file as it was drawn; check important dimensions against the official project documentation before relying on them.

Uninstalling

Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find CAD Touch Lite and choose Uninstall. Drawings that were converted from DWG stay in Documents\CAD Touch Lite until you delete them.
