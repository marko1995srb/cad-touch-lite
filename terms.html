Pro stays active until the end of the period you have already started (or of the free month); after that, viewing drawings and measuring one distance keep working, and your saved notes and measurements stay on your device. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.

What the app does

CAD Touch Lite opens, shows and measures DWG and DXF drawings, and lets you pin notes with text and pictures to them. It does not edit drawings: notes (and, on Android, saved measurements) are stored separately on your device and the drawing file is never changed. Measurements are calculated from the drawing file as it was drawn; check important dimensions against the official project documentation before relying on them.

Uninstalling

Windows: Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find CAD Touch Lite and choose Uninstall. Drawings that were converted from DWG stay in Documents\CAD Touch Lite until you delete them.

Android: Open Android Settings → Apps → CAD Touch Lite and choose Uninstall, or uninstall it in the Google Play app. This deletes the app's drawing copies, notes, measurements and pictures, unless you choose to keep the app's data when Android asks (Android 10 and newer). Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play.
