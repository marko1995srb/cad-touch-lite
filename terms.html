AI credits (Android)

1. What credits are. AI drawing review in the Android app uses credits. A check of one normal sheet usually uses 100 credits; a large or dense sheet or a whole model uses more (typically 150 to 400), because its analysis costs more. A standard check is usually ready within minutes and at the latest within 24 hours (a large area that is checked in parts: at the latest within 48 hours); a fast check is usually ready within a few minutes and uses 30% more credits. In these terms, a sheet and a page mean the same: one page of a drawing or of a PDF, or one picture.

2. How you get them.

  • Credit packs, bought in the app through Google Play: 100 credits, 600 credits, 2,000 credits, 10,000 credits or 55,000 credits. The price in your country is shown by Google Play before you pay; it is the price that applies.
  • Pro: a paid Pro subscription includes 200 credits each month. Credits included with Pro are valid until the end of that monthly period and do not carry over. When you check a drawing, included Pro credits are used before purchased credits. The free trial month of Pro includes no credits.
  • One free check: the first check of one sheet is free, once per installation of the app (see the privacy policy). The free check is available only in the app installed from Google Play, on a device that passes Google Play's integrity check. It shows 3 findings and the real number of findings of each kind; the other findings are kept on our server for 7 days, and within that time you can unlock them with the credits shown for that sheet, without a new analysis.

3. Purchased credits do not expire. Credits you have bought stay in your balance until you use them.

4. No money value, not transferable. Credits are not money and have no cash value. They cannot be exchanged for money, sold, given or transferred to another person, except for the refunds described in points 8 and 11.

5. Only in the Android app. Credits can be used only for AI drawing review (including the AI chat) in the CAD Touch app for Android in which they were bought through Google Play. They do not work in CAD Touch Lite for Windows or in CAD Touch for iPhone and iPad, which are separate products with separate purchases. AI drawing review is not offered in the Huawei AppGallery version.

6. You see the cost before you send. Before you tap Send, the app shows how many credits the check will use and how many you have. A check never uses more credits than the number you confirmed.

7. A check that is not finished uses no credits. When you send a check, its credits are reserved. If the check fails, expires or cannot be completed for a technical reason, the reserved credits go back to your balance automatically. The same applies if you cancel a check, even after its analysis has started, as long as its result has not reached our server; after that, the check can no longer be cancelled. A check whose result has reached our server uses its credits, whatever its findings are. To prevent abuse: after 5 cancelled checks on the same credit balance within 24 hours, a further check that you cancel after its analysis has started does not return its credits; the app tells you this before you cancel.

8. Refunds. Purchases are made through Google Play and Google Play's refund policies apply (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). You can also write to us at cadtouch.app@gmail.com with the order number from Google's receipt (it starts with "GPA."). If a purchase is refunded, the credits from that purchase are removed from your balance. If some of them were already used, your balance can go below zero; credits from your next purchase first cover that amount.

9. Lost or moved phone. Credits are kept on our server under a random installation ID, not under a name or account. If you change phones, uninstall the app or clear its storage, write to us with the order numbers of your purchases, and we will restore the unused credits from those purchases on your new installation.

  • Before the first question, the app scans the project. The scan uses credits per page, shown before you send, like a check.

What the app does

CAD Touch Lite opens, shows and measures DWG and DXF drawings, and lets you pin notes with text and pictures to them. It does not edit drawings: notes (and, on Android, saved measurements) are stored separately on your device and the drawing file is never changed. Measurements are calculated from the drawing file as it was drawn; check important dimensions against the official project documentation before relying on them.

Uninstalling

Windows: Open Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find CAD Touch Lite and choose Uninstall. Drawings that were converted from DWG stay in Documents\CAD Touch Lite until you delete them.

Android: Open Android Settings → Apps → CAD Touch Lite and choose Uninstall, or uninstall it in the Google Play app. This deletes the app's drawing copies, notes, measurements and pictures, unless you choose to keep the app's data when Android asks (Android 10 and newer). Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play.

iPhone and iPad: Touch and hold the CAD Touch icon, then choose Remove App › Delete App. This deletes the app's drawing copies, notes, measurements and pictures. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
